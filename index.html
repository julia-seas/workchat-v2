Resource Hub

Find Work & Start Earning

Are you new to the U.S. job market and looking for a job to cover everyday expenses while you figure out your next steps? Explore job options, get help with applications, and prepare for interviews.

Launch Your U.S. Career

Do you have professional qualifications and work experience from another country? Learn how to translate your qualifications for U.S. employers, navigate credential requirements, and land your first professional role in the U.S.

Advance or Change Your Career

Already working in your field and ready for more, or considering a different path? Explore resources to pursue a promotion, negotiate better pay, or transition into a new profession.

Start or Grow Your Business

Starting a business, freelancing, or growing your own practice? Get practical guidance on shaping your offer, setting prices, marketing your services, and attracting clients.

Our Stories

WorkChat has had a huge impact on my career journey.

I first joined as a participant, then had the opportunity to help administer online meetings, masterminds, and book clubs.

Over the years, WorkChat's helped me pivot my career, prepare for interviews with major tech companies, and stay informed about what's happening in the corporate world.

This is an incredible network of like-minded professionals who genuinely want to help each other succeed. I'm so grateful to the founders, Masha Makarenkova and Masha Boone, for creating such a supportive community.

Kristina E.
